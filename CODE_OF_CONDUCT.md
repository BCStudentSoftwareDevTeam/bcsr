# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Berea College Computer Science Department Code of
Conduct**, adapted from the [ACM Code of Ethics and Professional
Conduct](https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics). All contributors to this
repository — students, TAs, and faculty alike — are expected to uphold these
principles.

## I will...

1. Work to avoid causing harm and work to contribute to the good of society.
   I will support diversity, accessibility, and sustainability.
2. Be honest, trustworthy, and fair. I will not discriminate against any
   person or group of people. I will respect everyone's dignity online and
   in person.
3. Respect all people's work by crediting all sources and respecting all
   copyrights. I will not plagiarize.
4. Respect others' privacy and confidentiality. I will protect information
   and collect and share it only as necessary.
5. Insist on and support high-quality work and transparent communication
   from myself and others.
6. Maintain high standards of competence, conduct, ethical practice, and
   professionalism. I will strive for constant growth in order to meet
   these standards.
7. Respect existing laws and regulations pertaining to computing.
8. Accept and provide constructive review and critique.
9. Thoroughly evaluate the potential impacts and risks of my decisions,
   commitments, and work upon myself and upon others.
10. Share my understanding of computing with the public, addressing any
    misunderstandings.
11. Not access, alter, or prevent the access of someone else's computer,
    software, or data without authorization.
12. Design systems appropriately robust and secure for their intended
    purposes to the best of my ability. I will not release systems with
    known security flaws.
13. Recognize and take special care of systems that become integrated into
    the infrastructure of society.
14. Make people — including users, peers, and others affected directly or
    indirectly — a central concern in computing.
15. Uphold, promote, and respect the principles of this Code.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors to this project are expected to:

- Treat all contributors, maintainers, and users with respect and
  professionalism, regardless of experience level, background, or
  identity.
- Give proper credit for others' work, cite sources, and respect
  copyrights and licenses (Principles 3).
- Communicate honestly and transparently about the status, limitations,
  and risks of their work (Principles 2.1, 5, 9).
- Give and accept constructive, respectful code review and critique
  (Principle 8).
- Protect the privacy and confidentiality of any data handled by the
  project, collecting and sharing only what is necessary (Principle 4).
- Take security seriously: design features as robustly as reasonably
  possible and disclose known vulnerabilities rather than shipping them
  silently (Principle 12).

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Discrimination or harassment of any kind, including on the basis of age,
  disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, nationality, race,
  religion, sex, or sexual orientation.
- Plagiarizing code, documentation, or other work without attribution.
- Accessing, altering, or attempting to access another person's accounts,
  systems, or data without authorization — including this project's
  database, credentials, or deployment environment.
- Knowingly introducing security vulnerabilities, destructive code, or
  deceptive/fabricated data into the project.
- Dishonest conduct, such as misrepresenting the state of one's work,
  falsifying test results, or misattributing contributions.

## Community Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and for taking
fair, appropriate action — including requesting changes to a contribution
or declining a pull request — when this Code of Conduct is not upheld. All
contributors share responsibility for encouraging adherence to the Code
within the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior inconsistent with this Code of
Conduct within this repository (issues, pull requests, code review, or any
other project space), please report it to the project maintainers by
opening a confidential issue or contacting a repository admin directly on
GitHub.

Because this project is developed under the Berea College Computer Science
Department, violations may also be reported to the department, consistent
with Principle 15.2 of the Department Code of Conduct, and may be subject
to remedial action as specified in the Berea College Student Handbook.
